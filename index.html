About the scholarship

The scholarship recognises undergraduate and postgraduate students of the School of Computer Engineering who publish their research. Each year the School’s research committee reviews the papers its students have published, scores each one on the standing of the conference or journal, and awards the authors of the best. The aim is to encourage students to take their work to well-ranked venues early in their careers.

Recognition

Selected papers and their authors are recognised by the School.

Prize money

Awarded for each selected paper.

Better venues

The scoring rewards papers in well-ranked conferences.

For students

How it works

1

Publish

Publish your research as first author, with the School of Computer Engineering as your affiliation. Papers in Scopus-indexed conferences are preferred.

2

Apply

Send the details of your paper to the School in the format it asks for.

3

Selection and award

The School’s research committee scores each paper on its venue and citations.
