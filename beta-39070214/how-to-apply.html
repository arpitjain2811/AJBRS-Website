How to apply

Who is eligible, how to apply, and how papers are scored.

Eligibility

  • You are enrolled in the School of Computer Engineering at MIT, MAHE when the paper is submitted.
  • You are the first author, or a co-first author, of a published paper that lists the School of Computer Engineering as your affiliation.
  • Papers in Scopus-indexed conference proceedings are considered first, excluding conferences in their first year.
  • If there are no conference papers, Scopus-indexed open-access journal papers are considered.

Applying

  1. Send the School a formal application with the details of your published paper, in the format the School provides.
  2. The School's research committee, made up of the research coordinators and the Associate Deans, scores each paper with the rubric below. The rubric can change with the institute's research policies for the year.
  3. Awards go to the highest-scoring papers, up to the number of awards available that year.
